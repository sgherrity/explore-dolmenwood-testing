      About This Tool

      This tool is built for Dolmenwood Referees running sessions involving overland hex travel. Select a hex to see what is visible around the party.

      Visibility Model

      Eye Level = Hex Elevation + Vantage Height. A neighboring hex is visible if your eye level ≥ that hex's elevation + its obstruction height.

      Obstruction heights by terrain:

      • Tangled / Boggy / Hilly / Open Forest — ~60′
      • Craggy Forest — ~40′
      • Fungal Forest / Swamp — ~20′
      • Bog, Farmland, Meadow, Hills, Water — none
      Vantage Height

      Adjust the Vantage (ft) field to raise your eye level above the hex elevation. Reset returns vantage to the default height.

      Prominent entries, which are highlighted in red, are tall enough to be seen above the canopy even when a hex would otherwise be obscured.

      Controls
      • Player — strips hex information and is intended for sharing with players.
      • World — shows or hides the full Dolmenwood world map.
      • 2D — flattens elevation so all hexes are treated as equal height.
      Journeys

      Track the party’s overland travel day-by-day. Click Start Journey, pick a start date (Dolmenwood or real-world calendar), and click adjacent hexes to lay the trail. The console tracks your resources and the calendar as you go.

      • Travel Points / Miles — each day gives a Travel Point budget based on marching Speed; the bar shows remaining vs. total. 1 TP = 3 miles.
      • Wild / Path / River — travel cross-country, follow a road or trail, or move along a river (costs are drawn from the actual route geometry).
      • Rest — ends the day and restores the budget; after 6 days without a full rest day the party risks exhaustion.
      • Forced March — spend beyond the daily budget for extra distance.
      • Lost & Encounters — flagged automatically as you travel; both are dismissible.
      • Undo — steps back the last action (move, rest, forced march, lost/encounter) losslessly.

      From Saved Journeys you can Continue, Edit Origin (prepend), recolor, view the log, and Export / Import.

      Journeys are drawn on both maps as a colored line with waypoints marking the trail.

      Capture

      The Capture button exports the hex graphic as a high-resolution PNG — useful for sharing a sightline snapshot with players or dropping into session notes.
